# vv-contract

## Summary
V&V in this project is a three-layer ladder — unit beds with analytic ground truth (11 `test*` binaries under `src/applications/utilities/`), two order-of-accuracy verification cases under `verification/`, and ~75 `COMPARE.md` validation contracts under `validation/` — but only the bottom layer is actually gated. There is NO regression gate anywhere: `verification/*/report.py` never reads `results.baseline.txt`, nothing diffs them, `verification/` is entirely UNTRACKED in git, and the only pass/fail suite that exists (`tools/run_electrostatics_tests.sh`) covers electrostatics tutorials only. The COMPARE.md contract that CLAUDE.md rule A1 and `docs/working-with-claude.md` §2.6 describe as making the 2026-08-30 wrong-control failure "unreachable" is enforced by `tools/compare_cases.py` — which exists in `/home/kkourtza/Projects/SoEEDF/tools/` but **does not exist anywhere in the live SoPlasma tree**, has never existed there (`git log --all` finds nothing), and 23 of 75 SoPlasma `COMPARE.md` files lack the ```compare block it requires, so it would refuse them even if copied in. Two live verification artefacts are self-contradicting: `fluxScheme2Dnonortho/README.md`'s headline ("SG AND CFS DO NOT CONVERGE on a non-orthogonal mesh", order 0.00) quotes `results.baseline.txt` rows that all carry `nCorr=2` — the data from *before* its own documented harness fix — while the current `results.txt` on disk (`nCorr=15-16`) shows SG converging at p=0.97-1.00, and that file's `report.py` parses 0 of its 48 rows because the regex predates the `shear=` prefix. The validation layer (Grubert 2009 dc glow) has reproduced nothing: `docs/design/verification-map.md` states plainly that of 60 validation cases, 58 are Grubert variants and NONE has a known answer.

### The first Grubert LFA run passed the plateau check specified in its own COMPARE.md while being entirely invalid — a flat current is necessary for steady state and nowhere near sufficient.
**Evidence:** validation/grubert2009/COMPARE.md: 'The LFA arm ran to completion and reported a current density constant to 0.00% over 36 us, which is exactly what a converged steady state looks like. It was not one.' `tableKey meanE` had been inherited from the LMEA streamer bed, so under LFA the reducedE field (V m^2, ~2e-18) indexed tables tabulated against mean energy (eV, 0-2644). Every lookup hit the table floor, S_iz was 1.9e-210 against an expected 1.1e+20, and n_e stayed at 1.53e11 against the 1e11 floor where Grubert's LFA peak is 6.2e13. Now guarded: indexing a field into a table of a different physical quantity is fatal (plasmaTransport.C, 2026-09-06).

**Rule:** Before reading ANY current from a discharge run, assert two things: (1) n_e has risen WELL ABOVE minNumberDensity — a value pinned at the floor means the code never ignited; (2) S_iz is of order k_io * n_e * N, not 1e-210. Both checks come before the plateau test, not after it.

**Cost:** A converged-looking steady state that is a table-indexing bug, reported as a validation result.


### The named literature benchmarks and their current status are enumerated in docs/design/verification-map.md as a prioritised, costed ladder — none of the Tier 1-4 items is implemented; the Carlsson/JC-PIC GD benchmark comes with a documented ceiling on what is even achievable.
**Evidence:** verification-map.md, from DeChant 2023 (PSST 32 044006) and Teunissen 2020 (PSST 29 015010). Tier 1 A-E (linear chain eqs 26-31; argon ionisation + 3-body with a KNOWN steady state eqs 32-33; Debye screening eqs 34-35, exact V = V0 exp(-|x|/lambda_De); ambipolar parabola eqs 36-39; 0D particle balance eq 40). Tier 4 L, Carlsson et al. He benchmark: 3.5 Torr/300 K, gap 0.62 cm, cathode -211 V, gamma = 0.28, emitted-electron T 2 eV; JC-PIC matches EDIPIC and LSP to ~2% on E0 and ~0.02 cm on d_c, all three ~10% ABOVE the DenHartog/O'Brian/Lawler experimental E0. Suggested order: D, H, C, G, K, L. Paschen already measured V_b = 120.8 V for this gap.

**Rule:** Before proposing a new validation target, grep Literature/reference-codes-and-manuals/JC-PIC_Boeuf/library_article_book.txt (standing rule, user 2026-09-08) — every case there is a reproduction of a PUBLISHED benchmark with an honest account of what did and did not converge. Calibrate expectations against what the kinetic literature itself achieves: Carlsson's own 600 V case is UNRESOLVED even in JC-PIC's kinetic reproduction (did not converge at 150 us; needs ~10x longer, into the ms range, because the cold trapped negative-glow population builds on the ambipolar diffusion timescale, ~0.5 ms).

**Cost:** Declaring a short microsecond run 'failed to reach steady state' when the physics needs milliseconds; or targeting a benchmark that is open in the literature. Compounding this: SoPLASMA has NO electron-electron (Coulomb) collision term anywhere (docs/design/electron-electron-collisions-gap.md), the mechanism named as dominant for de-trapping that population — so a reproduction may plateau at the wrong bulk Te/density regardless of run length.


### On the Boltzmann side (SoEEDF) the V&V is genuinely layered and quantified, and §7 'What is not validated' is the honest inventory an agent should cite rather than the headline numbers.
**Evidence:** SoEEDF/docs/validation-summary.md. Layer 1: testPhysics, 89 assertions, no external data (SST identity alpha_bar*w - alpha_bar^2*D = nu_eff to better than 1e-4 of scale at 30/100/300/1000 Td; alpha/N - eta/N reproduces the eigenvalue to 1e-6). Layer 2: vs BOLSIG+, sub-0.25% on <eps>, muN, DN across 1-1000 Td in 0.16 s for ten solves; with Coulomb, within 0.5% on <eps> and mobility. Chemistry backends: worst relative difference 1.4e-15 on dn_s/dt, 0 (bit-identical) on 1 ns rodas23 integration, 6.7e-16 on the Jacobian. Gas heating vs Rusterholtz 2013 EXPERIMENT: deposited energy 1.025e6 vs 1.0532e6 J/m^3 (within 3%, uncalibrated), O(3P) 9.20e17 vs 9.0e17 +/-25% cm^-3, 6.96 vs 7.30 eV per O atom. Streamer (Bagheri PSST 27 095002): propagation speed 0.373 vs 0.5 mm/ns, ~25% low.

**Rule:** Quote §7 alongside any SoEEDF validation number. Specifically: the two-term approximation itself is NOT validated (every EEDF comparison is against another two-term solver); the Bagheri case is NOT run to its specification (our own cross sections and photoionization model), so the 25% speed gap is NOT ATTRIBUTABLE — '§5.1 calls it a data difference; that is a reasonable expectation, not a measurement'; only ONE quantity has a reference value; and NO spatial convergence study exists for the coupled case. Conclusion to state: the coupled validation demonstrates SELF-CONSISTENCY, NOT ACCURACY.

**Cost:** Reporting 'validated against a published streamer benchmark' when the benchmark's own inputs were not used and only one of three quoted quantities has a reference.


### Agreement between two source modes at a single dt is NOT a correctness check in an amplifying discharge; convergence of the GAP under refinement is. This was measured rather than explained away.
**Evidence:** SoEEDF/docs/validation-summary.md §5.3: implicitRate vs ode peak n_e differ by 0.6% at 1 ns and 31% at 2 ns, with the SIGN of the difference changing. Halving deltaT 1e-12 -> 5e-13: gap 0.0056->0.0014 (ratio 4.1) at 1.0 ns, 0.0839->0.0305 (2.8) at 1.5 ns, 0.3064->0.1685 (1.8) at 2.0 ns. The gap falls with dt at every time, so the paths are CONSISTENT and the difference is splitting error, not a bug. The ratio degrading from 4 toward 2 is the amplification itself.

**Rule:** When two formulations disagree in an amplifying discharge, refine dt and check whether the GAP shrinks. A constant gap under refinement means the formulations are inconsistent (a defect); a shrinking gap means two discretisations converging on the same solution. Never report single-dt agreement or disagreement as a verdict.

**Cost:** Either a false alarm (31% read as a bug) or a false clearance (agreement at one dt read as correctness).
